4. Copy deck (target vs now)

Use: Sign up / Log in · USD balance · Top up
Now in the prototype: Header Sign up / Log in. Default modal Login or sign up with email, Google / X / TikTok, Crypto wallet, Passkey, More options. Stripped sheet: ?minimal. USD balance on Account. Top up on the short-balance path and Account CTA. Do not invent: addresses, ETH, or tickers in the default Account hero for social users.

5. Out of scope

Fiat off-ramp and bank withdrawals · Moss wallet · Native app · Token creation form rework (the deploy modal after the form is in) · Interior of Privy and MoonPay hosted screens (theme values only, if we change them).

6. Dependencies

Q1 technical PRD: whether an external-wallet tip needs a signature per tip.
Sponsored gas: if yes, no “insufficient funds for fees” state.
USDm bonding curve: unfinished — affects how launchpad USD prices render.
